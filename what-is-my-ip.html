What Is an IP Address?

An IP address (Internet Protocol address) is a unique identifier assigned to your internet connection. Websites use it to send information back to your device and determine your approximate location.

Can Someone Track My IP Address?

Your IP address can reveal your internet provider and approximate city. While it does not reveal your exact home address, it can still be used to identify general location information.

How Can I Hide My IP Address?

A VPN routes your internet traffic through a secure server and replaces your visible IP address with another one. This helps improve privacy and security online.
