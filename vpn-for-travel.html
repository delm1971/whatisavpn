Before you travel

Install and test your VPN before departure. Check device compatibility and your destination’s local laws and network restrictions.

On the road

Choose nearby servers for performance and use a kill switch when appropriate. Some services may block VPN traffic.

Keep your accounts safe

Enable multifactor authentication, carry backup recovery codes securely and avoid relying solely on a VPN for account protection.
